================================================================================
KrakenD Community Edition (/usr/local/bin/krakend)
================================================================================

The container image redistributes the KrakenD Community Edition binary,
extracted from the upstream KrakenD image (https://hub.docker.com/_/krakend).
KrakenD CE is built on the Lura Project framework.
